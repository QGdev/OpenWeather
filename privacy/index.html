Privacy

OpenWeather has no account, no ads and no analytics. It is free software: everything below can be checked in the source code.

What stays on your phone

  • The places you saved and your settings.
  • Your OpenWeatherMap API key, stored encrypted.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

  • OpenWeatherMap (api.openweathermap.org): the coordinates or the name of each place you saved, your language and your API key, to get the weather and the air quality. Their privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the text you type when you search for a place to add. Their privacy policy.
  • Like any server, both see the IP address your request comes from.

Nothing else is sent. The app never asks for your location: you search for places by name. It requests the internet and network state permissions (to fetch and to know when to refresh) and vibration.

This website

This site sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. The sky shown on the page is kept in the address (the part after #), which your browser never sends to the server. The site makes no request to any third party. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any host, may log the IP address of visits: see GitHub's privacy statement.
